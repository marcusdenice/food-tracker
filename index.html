Food Database

This app's entire food list lives in one file, foods.csv, sitting right next to index.html in the repo. Download the current version below, edit it in a spreadsheet (add rows, change names, adjust numbers), then replace foods.csv in your GitHub repo with your edited version the same way you'd update any other file there. No conversion step, nothing to send back here - the page just loads whatever's in that file each time it opens.

Enter each nutrient amount as it appears on the label for that serving size - we'll convert everything to per-100g automatically when you save.

Nutrient values come from the USDA SR Legacy database. Vitamin/mineral targets use general adult RDAs - this is a planning tool, not a substitute for a dietitian, especially with kidney, thyroid, or other conditions where potassium/phosphorus intake needs individual attention. Iodine isn't tracked here. B12 and vitamin D are tracked, but vegan whole foods rarely provide them naturally, so keep an eye on those two bars and talk to a doctor about supplementing if they stay low.

*Marks the 9 essential amino acids - ones your body can't make on its own, so they have to come from food. Cystine and Tyrosine are "conditionally essential" instead (the body can make them from Methionine and Phenylalanine respectively, given enough of those), which is why they're unmarked. Also worth knowing: Methionine/Cystine and Phenylalanine/Tyrosine are officially scored by WHO/FAO as combined pairs, not individually - each amino acid in a pair is shown against that same shared target as a simplification, rather than two independently-derived numbers.
