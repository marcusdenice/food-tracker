Nutrient values come from the USDA SR Legacy database. Vitamin/mineral targets use general adult RDAs - this is a planning tool, not a substitute for a dietitian, especially with kidney, thyroid, or other conditions where potassium/phosphorus intake needs individual attention. Iodine and omega-3 fatty acids aren't tracked here; B12 and vitamin D are tracked, but vegan whole foods rarely provide them naturally, so keep an eye on those two bars and talk to a doctor about supplementing if they stay low.
